## Bugs found (new)

- **S-1 (high, FIXED on `gamification`, not deployed) Sound lab freezes.** Switching Open field ↔ Small room (or tabs) while a clap/shout/ping ring is still animating calls `setPlace()` → `clearRings()` (`mmaxr-webxr-client/src/lab/soundScene.ts:530`, `:572`). Rings are removed without calling `done`, so the awaited promise never resolves and `busy` stays `true`. From then on every Clap, Shout and Ping is silently ignored; the "Field and room" task cannot be completed without reloading (which starts a new session). Reproduced; waiting ~4 s between clap and room switch avoids it. Fix: resolve pending `done` callbacks in `clearRings()` (or reset `busy` there).
- **S-2 (scoring design) Sound double-penalises a calculation slip.** A sonar depth calc needing 2 tries lowers causal coherence (`calcFirst`) and also zeroes that task in transfer (`calcAttempts <= 1`), even though the prediction was right.
- **M-1 (low) Metals scorer can count more improvements than wrong first conclusions** (improved 4 > wrongFirst 3, ratio > 1, not clamped) when the page's first impression differs from the recorded `set_conclusion`. Seen after a second impression dialog was opened over the first (automation; hard for a mouse user). Score unaffected because `band()` caps at 3.
- **L-1 Light "You predicted / What happened" lines lag one question** (law and ray results show the Phase 1 foil text or the previous question's "70°"/"32°"). Known as L8-a, still present.
- **L-2 Light concave/convex "Explain it (optional)" says "Your prediction differed from the trace" even when all 4 fields matched.**
- **G-1 Lab overlays leak between labs** (Chemistry reveal stayed in the Magnetism DOM; Metals reveal sat on top of Cell Explorer and blocked canvas clicks). Known L4-b, still present.
- **Mg-1 Magnetism Electromagnetism Q1 begins "The same wire and compass…"** but no earlier question set it up.
- **F-1 Fundamentals typo:** "Watch the a battery torch on the energy bench."
- **C-1 Cell sessions abandoned by closing the tab never appear** on the dashboard (no `leave_lab`, filtered by `ENDED`).
- Still present from 2026-11-25: coach in basic mode with "You changed your mind there…" / "Stuck?" nudges repeating (also after choosing Off); "That may have surprised you" on matched trials (L4-c); `lessonType: physics_motion` for Electricity, Magnetism, Fundamentals, Simple Machines and Matter (L4-e); Sound task text "running at 4 Hz" while the instrument shows 3 Hz (L11-a) and raw variable names in the Sound summary (L11-b); Motion Newton Q5 can deal "30 kg and 30 kg" with two identical options.
- Verified fixed: Biology "Next stop" without watching is refused (stays on the stop); sensor data now uploads (`hasSensorData: yes` in every non-biology lab).

## Notes on the method

- Retrieval effort here is mostly set by how fast the script clicked Run after locking (1–8 s) and pointer travel is ~0 because clicks are synthetic, so RE values are not meaningful as "student" behaviour, only as a check that the rule was applied.
- Explanations were typed (the mic is blocked in the test browser, which unlocks typing).
- Abandoned sessions left in the DB: Sound `sess_4baf8fdc` (left), one Cell session never ended.
