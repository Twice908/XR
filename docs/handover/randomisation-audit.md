## Findings that matter for the later tasks

1. **No seeding anywhere.** Every draw uses `Math.random()` (`drawVariants`, `makePlan` in `optics.ts` and `sound.ts`, `pickSolutions`, `pickMaterials`). A session is only stable because the assignment is stored in Redis for 24 h. After the TTL a reload re-deals.
2. **Three separate engines.** `motion.ts` (six lessons), `optics.ts` and `sound.ts` each have their own shuffle and variant picker, and chemistry and metals have their own samplers. Task 2 should collapse these.
3. **Variants are hand-written, not derived.** Motion-family variants are generated by `scripts/gen-*.py`, but each variant stores its `answer` as data. Only `sound.ts` (`derive`) computes answers from parameters. There is no check that a stored answer matches its stated values (task 6d).
4. **Answer position is not balanced.** Options are shuffled independently per question, so a student can get a run of the same letter. Over 1000 draws the distribution will be near uniform, but nothing enforces it per session.
5. **Pool sizes are borderline.** Motion-family: min 5 variants per question, so the >= 5 rule passes with no headroom. Optics and sound have 8 variants, but optics spherical cases (6 concave, 2 convex) have no variants and are only reordered. Numeric questions: matter 2, circuits 4, fundamentals 18, motion 6, machines 6, magnetism 2. I have not yet checked that each numeric question has 5 *distinct* value sets.
6. **No history across attempts.** Assignments are keyed by `sessionId` only. A retake in a new session can repeat variants, and no per-student record exists.
7. **Biology is fixed end to end.** Checkpoint prompts, the multi-select option lists (`choices`) and transfer options are identical for every student, and the transfer options are not shuffled. The only per-student state is their commits.
8. **Post-reveal answers are intentional.** The client reads `o.answer` (`motionHud.ts:273-275`) and `reveal.answer` (`biologyHud.ts`, `biologyLab.ts`) only after the server's observe/reveal response. `state/metals.ts:284` compares hypotheses against `t.type`, which arrives after testing. These are by design, not leaks. A live capture should confirm that no pre-commit response includes them.
9. **`params` and `concept` are public in the motion family** (`redactMotion` passes them through). They drive the animation and the concept card. The per-variant `params` carry the question's numbers, which is intended, but a variant author could put derived answers there. A test for this belongs in task 6e.
10. **Metals `factsFor` / `why`** are revealed after submission only. Fine.

## Proposed scope decisions for the user

- **physics-101 (Bohr):** it has no questions today. I suggest leaving it out of this work and handling it as its own lesson build. Decision needed.
- **Optics convex cases (2) and plane scenarios (2):** need new variants to reach 5.
- **Biology:** the "variants" there should be alternative phrasings of the checkpoint prompts and a larger transfer-question pool dealt N of M, not alternative anatomy. Decision on how much new content to write is needed before task 4.
